Rules
Prioritise original reporting about significant new developments published within the past 48 hours. Exclude sponsored content, shopping guides, evergreen advice and unsubstantiated rumours. Verify publication dates. Where an article summarises another publisher’s reporting, seek the original source. Merge coverage of the same event into one story; list up to three sources, original first.

HOW TO JUDGE A STORY
Rate each story Very low / Low / Medium / High / Very high for Sport, Fashion and Marketing relevance.

WHERE EACH STORY GOES
- The Intersection: only if the story scores Medium or higher in ALL THREE (Sport, Fashion and Marketing).
- Otherwise: place the story in the section where it scores highest.
- A story can appear in only one section.

We value:
- Cultural significance: big names, iconic brands, people others look up to
- "Firsts" and shifts that shake up an industry
- Progress in women's sport
- Unconventional, creative marketing
- Career moves and progression in marketing leadership
- Timeliness, especially stories that put readers ahead of the curve
We reject:
- Think pieces and evergreen filler written just to fill a website
- Reaction videos and routine club content
- Small clubs or high-street brands doing ordinary things
- Routine announcements (e.g. store openings) with nothing striking.

Exclusions
* Press releases, sponsored or "partner" content, advertorials and affiliate shopping pieces ("10 best trainers to buy").
* Listicles, quizzes, galleries and "what we're loving this week" round-ups.
* Opinion or comment pieces, unless the source is a named expert and the piece reports new information.
* Celebrity sightings, outfits or personal life stories with no business, sporting or creative substance.
* Live blogs, video reactions, match reports with only scores, and minute-by-minute updates.
* Articles published more than 48 hours before {{date}}, or any article whose publication date you cannot confirm.
* Updates to older stories that add nothing new.
* Broken links
* Sources from aggregators, forums, social media posts and AI-generated content farms.
* Content involving graphic violence, or tragedies treated as marketing angles.
